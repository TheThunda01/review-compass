References and rationale

Sources behind the planning guidance.

Review Compass is an educational planning tool. It summarizes reliable methods guidance in plain language, but students should still consult the original sources, mentors, and librarians before using a plan for a formal protocol or manuscript.

Terms used in the guidance

PRISMA means Preferred Reporting Items for Systematic reviews and Meta-Analyses; PRISMA-S concerns searching, and PRISMA-ScR concerns scoping reviews. PRESS means Peer Review of Electronic Search Strategies. JBI and Cochrane publish evidence-synthesis methods. GRADE means Grading of Recommendations Assessment, Development and Evaluation. PICO, PCC, SPIDER, and CMO are explained on the review-types page. A record is a database entry, a report is a document, and a study is an investigation.
