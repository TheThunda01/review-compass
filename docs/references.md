## Research and methodological articles

[DOI: 10.1186/s13643-020-01542-z](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13643-020-01542-z). PMID: [33499930](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33499930/).

Used here: PRISMA-S specifies search-reporting items; it does not validate a search or this app. Access: abstract. Current appraisal used the PubMed abstract, not the full article or supplements. Consensus reporting guideline, not a performance trial.

<a id="pmid-40926967"></a>

<a id="pmid-33782057"></a>
**Matthew J Page et al. (2021).** The PRISMA 2020 statement: an updated guideline for reporting systematic reviews. *BMJ (Clinical research ed.)*, 372, n71. [DOI: 10.1136/bmj.n71](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj.n71). PMID: [33782057](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33782057/).

Used here: PRISMA 2020 is reporting guidance, with checklists and flow diagrams. Access: abstract. Current appraisal used the PubMed abstract, not the full article or supplements. Reporting completeness alone does not establish unbiased review conduct.

<a id="pmid-30178033"></a>

Used here: PRISMA-ScR provides 20 essential and two optional scoping-review reporting items. Access: abstract. Current appraisal used the PubMed abstract, not the full article or supplements. Reporting guidance is not a substitute for a scoping-review method.

<a id="pmid-27005575"></a>

<a id="pmid-38911529"></a>

<a id="pmid-33076832"></a>

## Guidance, standards, and software

<a id="cochrane-05"></a>
**Li T, Higgins JPT, Deeks JJ (2024).** [Chapter 5: Collecting data](https://www.cochrane.org/authors/handbooks-and-manuals/handbook/current/chapter-05). 6.5. Official source; accessed 2026-10-20.

Used here: Studies and reports are distinct; extraction should retain their relationships. Structured, piloted forms and planned handling of disagreements support transparent extraction. Chapter last updated October 2019; handbook citation is version 6.5 (2024). Guidance does not evaluate either project.

<a id="cochrane-14"></a>
**Schünemann HJ et al. (2024).** [Chapter 14: Completing Summary of findings tables and grading the certainty of the evidence](https://www.cochrane.org/authors/handbooks-and-manuals/handbook/current/chapter-14). 6.5. Official source; accessed 2026-10-20.

Used here: GRADE evaluates certainty by outcome across a body of evidence; populated cells do not establish certainty. Chapter last updated August 2023; guidance is for intervention evidence, not a software quality score.

<a id="asreview-repository"></a>
**ASReview developers (date not specified).** [ASReview LAB repository](https://github.com/asreview/asreview). README accessed 2026-10-20. Official source; accessed 2026-10-20.

Used here: The project implements model-assisted prioritization using human screening labels. Repository README advertised version 3 when checked; the cited 2025 paper evaluates version 2. No code or performance is transferred to these projects.

<a id="cochrane-02"></a>
**Thomas J et al. (2024).** [Chapter 2: Determining the scope of the review and the questions it will address](https://www.cochrane.org/authors/handbooks-and-manuals/handbook/current/chapter-02). 6.5. Official source; accessed 2026-10-20.

Used here: Systematic reviews can have broad or narrow questions; purpose and feasibility guide scope. Chapter updated August 2023.

<a id="cochrane-03"></a>
**McKenzie JE et al. (2024).** [Chapter 3: Defining the criteria for including studies and how they will be grouped for the synthesis](https://www.cochrane.org/authors/handbooks-and-manuals/handbook/current/chapter-03). 6.5. Official source; accessed 2026-10-20.

Used here: Plan eligibility and synthesis together; outcome reporting should not routinely be an inclusion requirement. Chapter updated August 2023; context is intervention reviews.

<a id="cochrane-04"></a>
**Lefebvre C et al. (2025).** [Chapter 4: Searching for and selecting studies](https://www.cochrane.org/authors/handbooks-and-manuals/handbook/current/chapter-04). 6.5.1. Official source; accessed 2026-10-20.

Used here: Use appropriate information sources, text terms and subject headings, and retain search details; consult an information specialist. Chapter updated March 2025; handbook citation is 6.5.1, unlike several other chapters.

<a id="cochrane-08"></a>
**Higgins JPT et al. (2024).** [Chapter 8: Assessing risk of bias in a randomized trial](https://www.cochrane.org/authors/handbooks-and-manuals/handbook/current/chapter-08). 6.5. Official source; accessed 2026-10-20.

Used here: RoB 2 assesses specific randomized-trial results using domain judgments and written reasons. Chapter updated October 2019; not an appraisal tool for every study design.

<a id="cochrane-10"></a>
**Deeks JJ et al. (2024).** [Chapter 10: Analysing data and undertaking meta-analyses](https://www.cochrane.org/authors/handbooks-and-manuals/handbook/current/chapter-10). 6.5. Official source; accessed 2026-10-20.

Used here: Meta-analysis statistically combines results; compatibility, bias and heterogeneity require judgment. Chapter updated November 2024; the app performs no meta-analysis.
